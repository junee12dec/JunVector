# 캐릭터 일관성 유지 디자인 시트 프롬프트 2탄

> 레퍼런스 이미지 1장 + 프롬프트 → 다각도 디자인 시트 1장 완성
> 가끔 틀리는 부분만 수정하면 끝!

---

## 캐릭터용 프롬프트

```
A professional character design sheet in the same 2D digital illustration style, with a pure white background and 2K resolution. The character is a young East Asian female, matching the one in the original illustration in the image 1, straight black hair with a short bob cut, and arms crossed in a strong posture. The sheet includes:

HEAD DETAILS SECTION:
 * Front view of head without accessories (clear face shot)
 * Right side view of head without accessories
 * Front view of head with hair and full outfit
 * Right side view of head with hair and full outfit
   All head views must have identical facial features, skin tone, and proportions, with no perspective distortion.

FULL BODY ORTHOGRAPHIC SECTION:
5 full body views arranged horizontally:
 * Front view
 * Left 45-degree side view
 * Right side view
 * Right 45-degree side view
 * Back view
   All views must be of equal width and height, in orthographic projection with no perspective distortion. The character wears an orange knit long-sleeve dress and no accessories. She maintains a strong, neutral standing pose, full body in frame, with arms naturally hanging at her sides in all five views (replacing the crossed-arm pose for better clarity).

SUPPLEMENTARY VIEWS SECTION:
 * Half-body side view
 * Top-down overhead view
 * Bottom-up under view
   Character, outfit, and proportions must match the main views exactly.

MATERIAL DETAIL SECTION:
Extreme close-up shots of:
 * Fabric texture of the orange knit dress (clear stitch patterns)
 * Back view of the neck seam and dress details
   Clear display of material patterns, textures, and fabric details.

OVERALL CONSISTENCY REQUIREMENTS:
 * All modules show the same character with identical facial features, height, body type, and outfit.
 * High precision, maximum detail, cinematic animation character design standard.
 * Professional photography lighting with soft, even shadows.
 * Clean white background.
 * No overlapping or cropping of modules.
 * The design sheet should be organized in a precise grid layout with clear spacing and standardized labels, mirroring a professional production design document.
```

## 강아지용 프롬프트 (2마리 동시)

```
A professional character design sheet in a photorealistic style, pure white background, 2K resolution. The subjects are two dogs, based on the reference image:
- A small black Cocker Spaniel with curly fur, long floppy ears, and soft expressive eyes
- A medium-sized cream/gray Jindo-type dog with upright ears and a dense double coat

GLOBAL CONSISTENCY RULES (CRITICAL)
- Both dogs must appear in every frame
- Both dogs must face the exact same orientation and angle in each frame
- Maintain consistent scale, spacing, and proportions
- Clean orthographic projection where specified (no distortion)
- Neutral studio lighting, soft shadows

HEAD & FACIAL EXPRESSION SECTION
Both dogs shown side-by-side in each frame:
Angles (matched for both dogs):
- Front view / Right side view / Left 45-degree view
Expressions (same set for both dogs):
- Neutral / Alert (ears engaged) / Happy (slightly open mouth) / Curious (subtle head tilt)
All views must preserve identical facial structure, fur pattern, and proportions.

FULL BODY ORTHOGRAPHIC SECTION
5 full body views arranged horizontally (both dogs included in each):
- Front view / Left 45-degree view / Right side view / Right 45-degree view / Back view
Requirements:
- Both dogs rotate together identically
- Neutral standing pose, full body visible (including paws and tail)
- No perspective distortion

SIMPLIFIED DETAIL SECTION (ONE IMAGE EACH)
- Paw Detail: front and rear paws of both dogs together, including paw pads and claws
- Ear Detail: both ear types side-by-side (long floppy vs upright)
- Fur Detail: both fur types side-by-side (curly black vs dense double coat), texture and direction

SUPPLEMENTARY VIEWS SECTION
- Half-body side view (both dogs aligned)
- Top-down overhead view
- Bottom-up under view

OVERALL PRESENTATION
- Clean grid layout with labeled sections
- No overlapping or cropping
- Equal spacing and consistent framing
- Ultra-detailed, photorealistic quality suitable for professional animation or VFX production
```

## 사용법

1. AI 이미지 생성 도구(ChatGPT, Midjourney 등) 열기
2. 레퍼런스 이미지 1장 업로드
3. 위 프롬프트 붙여넣기 후 "레퍼런스 이미지 기준으로 맞춤 수정해줘" 추가
4. 틀린 부분만 인페인트·수정으로 보완
