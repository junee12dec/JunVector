# 글자 속 도시 — 3D 타이포그래피 다이오라마 나노바나나 프롬프트

> 출처: Threads @jeju_harry
> 도시를 입력하면 글자 미니어처를 만들어주는 나노바나나 프롬프트. 예전 타이포그래피 버전을 개선한 업그레이드판.

## 사용법

`[INPUT CITY NAME]` 자리에 원하는 도시명(한글 또는 영문)을 넣으면 된다.
예시: `[토토로]`, `[PARIS]`, `[제주]`

---

## 프롬프트 전문

```
[INPUT CITY NAME]: [여기에 도시명 입력]

Create a premium 3D typographic city artwork in the form of a richly decorated micro-world typography diorama, where the INPUT CITY NAME itself is the entire inhabited world.

[LANGUAGE LOCK — ABSOLUTE]
Render the typography using INPUT CITY NAME exactly as written, character by character.
If the input is Korean, the output must be Korean Hangul characters.
If the input is English or Latin alphabet, the output must be English letters.
Do not translate, romanize, localize, abbreviate, or substitute.
Any language conversion is an error.
Treat the INPUT CITY NAME as a physical sculptural object, not as a word.

[TYPOGRAPHY IS WORLD — ABSOLUTE]
The city name itself must be the world, not text with decorations placed on top.
Architecture, terrain, streets, foliage, water, props, and local objects must be structurally integrated into the letterforms themselves.
The letters must feel carved, built, planted, eroded, inhabited, and alive from within.

[ABSOLUTE AUTHENTICITY CONSTRAINT]
All decorative elements must be exclusively authentic to the selected city and its immediate region.
Do not include any landmarks, symbols, architecture, plants, vehicles, materials, objects, or visual styles from other cities or cultures.
If authenticity is uncertain, omit the element entirely.

[PRIMARY VISUAL INTENT — NON-NEGOTIABLE]
The typography must be heavily decorated, densely filled, and visually overwhelming in richness.
Plain, clean, minimalist, lightly decorated, or logo-like letters are not allowed.
The result must not resemble branding, poster typography, title design, or a graphic logo.
It must feel like a collectible premium miniature city sculpture captured as cinematic 3D art.

[DENSITY / COVERAGE — HARD REQUIREMENTS]
Each character must contain many miniature elements, in layered clusters and overlapping micro-scenes.
The combined decorations should visually cover 70–90% of each character's visible surfaces, including front faces, top planes, inner cavities, edges, corners, and embedded areas.
Do not leave any broad clean letter surfaces or large empty faces.
If an area feels empty, fill it with city-authentic micro-architecture, terraces, stairs, foliage, stonework, pathways, railings, tiles, local props, or texture detail.

[LETTER-BY-LETTER HERO SCENE]
Every character must contain its own distinct hero vignette based on authentic local identity.
Each character should reward close inspection with different clusters of landmarks, micro-streets, small structures, plants, props, terrain features, and local textures.
Do not decorate only one or two characters; every character must feel complete and alive.

[DECORATION PLACEMENT — MANDATORY]
Decorate both the exterior surfaces and the interior cavities or cutouts of every character.
Add micro-landmarks, tiny buildings, miniature streets, stairs, alleys, bridges, fences, railings, lanterns, signboards, stalls, markets, boats, vehicles, water channels, retaining walls, stone details, utility elements, local plants, trees, flowers, and repeated micro-details.
Place elements on top, inside, along edges, wrapped around corners, hanging from overhangs, inset into surfaces, and embedded directly into the material of the typography.
Use repeated small elements such as many trees, flowers, lanterns, windows, paving stones, tiles, fences, signs, and steps to increase richness and diorama density.

[NO CLEAN FACE RULE]
Do not leave any major front-facing letter plane smooth, blank, or untextured.
Break up all major surfaces with embedded structures, windows, balconies, terraces, stairs, vegetation, paving, stonework, signage, layered materials, or micro-topography.

[READABILITY RULE]
Despite extreme decoration density, the silhouette of each character must remain clearly readable from a medium distance.
At first glance, the viewer must clearly read the city name.
At second glance, the viewer must discover an overwhelming amount of miniature local life packed into every character.

[MICRO-SCALE READABILITY]
Include tiny scale-reference elements such as people, bicycles, small vehicles, steps, bridges, boats, stalls, pathways, benches, railings, lamps, and doors so the viewer instantly understands the monumental-to-miniature contrast.

[INSTANT LOCAL MOOD]
The viewer should recognize the city's local atmosphere at a glance through city-specific terrain, vegetation, materials, color palette, climate cues, and spatial mood, even before identifying individual landmarks.

[COMPOSITION]
The city name must fill most of the frame and remain centered.
Use a front-dominant semi-isometric hero angle that preserves immediate readability of the full city name while still revealing top surfaces, inner cavities, and layered depth.
Background must remain clean, soft, minimal, and unobtrusive.
All visual complexity must stay concentrated on the typography itself.

[VISUAL HOOKS]
Add concentrated eye-catching moments such as warm lantern clusters, reflective water surfaces, glowing windows, glossy foliage highlights, tiny bridges, colorful stalls, layered pathways, and small light-catching details that guide the eye across the full typography.

[LIGHTING & MATERIALS]
Use soft cinematic lighting with warm highlights and gentle shadows that emphasize depth between mini-elements.
Add subtle rim lighting to separate the characters from the background.
Use realistic materials and micro-textures such as wood grain, stone, metal, concrete, roof tiles, plaster, foliage, water, fabric, weathering, and surface wear.
Lighting must enhance dimensionality, richness, and premium diorama realism without flattening the micro-details.

[STYLE & RENDERING]
Octane Render,
Cinema 4D style,
front-dominant semi-isometric diorama typography,
ultra-detailed layered miniature set design,
living ecosystem,
cinematic premium 3D sculpture,
soft global illumination,
smooth light projection,
8K resolution,
high micro-detail fidelity,
collector-grade craftsmanship,
dense local storytelling.

[GLOBAL NEGATIVE PROMPT]
Plain typography,
clean typography,
lightly decorated letters,
minimalist text-only design,
logo-style typography,
branding look,
poster-title look,
large empty letter surfaces,
few props,
sparse decoration,
generic landmarks,
non-authentic landmarks,
landmarks from other cities,
foreign architecture or plants,
English typography when input is Korean,
romanized city names,
translated city names,
fantasy elements,
random cultural mixing,
flat lighting,
low detail,
graphic design style,
clean front faces,
undecorated cavities,
empty inner cutouts.

Generate a visually explosive, authenticity-driven, densely inhabited city-name sculpture where every character feels like a complete miniature world, instantly readable as the city name from afar and endlessly rewarding on close inspection.
```
